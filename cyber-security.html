Cyber security

▣ Print

Cybersecurity is identified and treated as high risk to DPE, with the risk to the confidentiality of our data, security of information, availability of IT Systems and most importantly impact to our offshore operations. We work closely with local government agencies, industry peers and partners while leveraging industry best practices to understand and respond to emerging cyber threats.

In addition to Advanced Threat Protection and Technology, our employees also play a key role in protecting against these attacks and we promote secure behaviours to help mitigate this growing risk. This user awareness is critical as we remain vigilant as the threat continues to evolve.
